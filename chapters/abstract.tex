For an undergraduate thesis abstract, write one paragraph (150–250 words) that briefly summarizes your project. Begin by stating the background or problem you are addressing and the objective of your study. Then, describe the methods you used to carry out the research. Next, summarize the main results or findings. Finally, conclude by explaining the significance of your results or the potential application of your work. Keep the writing clear and simple, avoid detailed explanations or references, and use past tense for work you have completed.
